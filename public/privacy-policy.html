Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Social Content Publisher application ("the Application") handles information when you use it to authorize and publish content to TikTok.

1. About the Application

Social Content Publisher is a personal web application developed by Rahul Sewa for managing and publishing personal video content to TikTok using TikTok's developer APIs. The Application is not a commercial social-media management service.

2. Information We Access

When you authorize the Application through TikTok, the Application may receive information made available through TikTok's authorization system and the permissions granted to the Application. This may include:

  • TikTok account identification information;
  • Authorization and access tokens required to communicate with TikTok;
  • Information required to publish content to the authorized TikTok account; and
  • Content and publishing information necessary to complete a publishing request.

The Application only requests permissions necessary for its implemented functionality.

3. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through TikTok is used only to authenticate the authorized TikTok account, establish an authorized connection with TikTok, publish video content to the authorized TikTok account, and operate, troubleshoot, and maintain the Application. Information obtained through TikTok is not used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

4. Data Sharing

The Application does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information obtained through TikTok is not shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the Application or where required by law. The Application uses TikTok's APIs to perform actions that you explicitly authorize.

5. Data Retention

The Application retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide and maintain its functionality. Authorization tokens and related information may be stored when required for authenticated API operations. Such information is protected using reasonable technical and organizational measures.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect application credentials, authorization information, and other data handled by the Application. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Revoking Access

You can revoke the Application's authorization through the applicable TikTok account settings or authorization controls provided by TikTok. After authorization is revoked, the Application will no longer be able to use the revoked authorization to access TikTok services.

8. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under the applicable minimum age required to use TikTok or the Application.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Application's functionality or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
